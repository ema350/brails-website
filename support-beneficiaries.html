Beneficiaries with Brails

Frequently asked questions about beneficiaries. 2 articles

  1. Click Beneficiaries in the left navigation.
  2. Click Add beneficiaries on the right of the page.
  3. Select a country (the currency is filled in for you).
  4. Select the payment method and click Next.
  5. Enter the beneficiary’s payment details accurately.
  6. Click Add beneficiary. You’re done!

You can also add a beneficiary while making a transfer: Wallets → Transfer → Select beneficiary → Add new beneficiary. If the country has only one payment method, the currency and method are filled in for you.
