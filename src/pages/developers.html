Controls for the people who run the integration.

API key management
Create, rotate and revoke keys from the dashboard.

      
{{icon:server}}ip.allow()
IP whitelisting
Only your servers can use your keys.

      
{{icon:lock}}auth.verify()
2FA and passkeys
Protect the dashboard that holds the keys.

      
{{icon:list}}log.list()

Every action in the dashboard is recorded, with who did it and when.
