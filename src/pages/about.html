About Brails

We’re building the rails African businesses trade on.

Brails is payments infrastructure for businesses that buy, sell and pay across borders: one account to pay suppliers abroad and hold the currencies they trade in.

Why we exist

Trading across borders shouldn’t be the hard part.

Paying across borders is still slower, costlier and harder to follow than it should be for the businesses doing the trading.

We built Brails so a business can see what a payment will cost before it commits, hold the currencies it needs, and track every step to settlement.
