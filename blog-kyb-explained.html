KYB explained: what we ask for and why

What business verification involves and how to get through it faster.

Before a business can send money through Brails, we verify it. This is called KYB, or Know Your Business. It can feel like paperwork, but it is what keeps the platform safe for every business that uses it, including yours.

Why we verify every business

As a licensed money transfer operator, we are required to know who we are moving money for. KYB helps us prevent money laundering and terrorist financing, and it protects your account from being opened or used by someone pretending to be you.

What we ask for

The details fall into a few groups:

  • Your representative: name, date of birth, nationality, position and contact details
  • Your business: name, registration number, tax number, business type and country of registration
  • Your address: where the business operates
  • Your stakeholders: directors, and anyone who owns more than 25% of the business
  • Documents that confirm the above

Documents by business type

Business typeTypical documents
Sole proprietorshipProof of address, proprietor’s ID, business name certificate, 3 months of business bank statements
Limited companyProof of address, shareholder IDs, certificate of incorporation, registers of directors and shareholders, memorandum and articles, 3 months of statements
Incorporated trusteesProof of address, trustee IDs, certificate of registration, constitution, account statement

The full list for each business type is in our Support Centre under Verification.

How to get through it faster

  • Scan documents clearly, in colour, with all four corners visible.
  • Make sure names and addresses match across documents.
  • Use statements from the last three months.
  • List every stakeholder who owns more than 25% at the start.

Once verification is complete, every feature on the platform opens up for your business.
